# Design and UX skills

The Motchi brand system is the source of truth. Keep approved colors, monograms, interaction patterns, and branch identities; use skill guidance with judgment.

- `frontend-design`: shape visual hierarchy, composition, typography, and distinctive page direction. Use for heroes and sections; do not override approved brand decisions or add novelty for its own sake.
- `apple-design`: improve continuity, easing, responsive feedback, and transitions. Use for hover, focus, selection, loading, and disclosures; do not make Motchi look like Apple. Respect reduced motion.
- `design-systems-frontend-architecture`: guide shared components, tokens, spacing, variants, and maintainability across MS, MW, and MC. Do not force the branches to look identical.
- `ux-writing`: improve clarity and human language in labels, buttons, forms, and supporting copy. Remove repetition; do not rewrite approved copy unnecessarily.
- `web-design-guidelines`: use as a responsive, accessibility, interaction, form, and usability QA layer. Check findings in context rather than mechanically overriding the brand.
- `hallmark`: audit repeated macrostructures, generic cards, redundant eyebrows, excessive copy, and unsupported proof. Use selectively to improve structure; do not remove every eyebrow, redesign every page, or treat its rules as absolute.

## Recommended workflow

1. Use `hallmark` to identify structural repetition and weak content.
2. Use `frontend-design` to develop the composition and hierarchy.
3. Use `design-systems-frontend-architecture` to keep implementation reusable and maintainable.
4. Use `ux-writing` to clarify the content without needless rewriting.
5. Use `apple-design` to refine interaction and motion.
6. Use `web-design-guidelines` for responsive, accessibility, and usability QA.

Apply only the skills relevant to the work. User instructions and the approved Motchi brand system take precedence over skill defaults.
